## Sector Overview & Market Dynamics

The global Digital Experience Platform (DXP) and portal software market is estimated at approximately **$16B–$19B in 2025–2026**, growing at a double-digit CAGR toward over $30B by the early 2030s. The sector exhibits a bifurcated market structure: it is **moderately concentrated** at the top-tier enterprise level—dominated by hyperscale and suite incumbents like Salesforce, Adobe, and Sitecore capturing large recurring contracts—while the broader ecosystem remains **highly fragmented** across composable headless CMS engines, mid-market website/portal builders, and specialized community platforms.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Richie](https://github.com/openfun/richie)**  

  Open-source CMS for building education portals (MIT). 316 GitHub stars, 95 forks. Built with Python/Django by openfun. Features course catalog management, organization pages, and person profiles. Actively maintained with 3,510 commits from 36 contributors .



- **[Gatewaze](https://github.com/gatewaze/gatewaze)**  

  Modular open-source platform for communities (license varies). Features members, events, content, and communications. Admin app at port 5274, public portal at port 3100. Setup wizard guides platform naming, administrator account creation, module selection, and theme choice. Supports Supabase (self-hosted or cloud) with Kubernetes Helm charts .



- **[Ibexa Experience](https://github.com/ibexa/experience)**  

  Modern modular DXP (license varies) for customer-centric organizations. Goes beyond content management to create websites and digital experiences . Ibexa Headless combines headless CMS, DAM, and PIM for API-first multichannel delivery; Ibexa Commerce is B2B DXP from customer awareness to purchase .



- **[sivujetti](https://github.com/sivujetti/sivujetti)**  

  Self-hosted website builder and CMS (license varies). Lightweight page builder for creating websites without SaaS dependencies .



- **[Visually](https://github.com/Git002/Visually)**  

  Drag-and-drop website builder that generates clean code (license varies). Gives users freedom to host their website anywhere. Built with Svelte .



- **[AntCMS](https://github.com/AntCMS-org/AntCMS)**  

  Super lightweight and simple CMS built for the modern web and simplicity (license varies) .



- **[p3ol/oak](https://github.com/p3ol/oak)**  

  Modern, lightweight and modulable page builder (license varies). 35 GitHub stars .



- **[Grav](https://github.com/getgrav/grav)**  

  Modern, fast flat-file CMS powered by PHP, Markdown, Twig, and Symfony (MIT). 14,260 GitHub stars, 1,376 forks. No database required, making it ideal for simple portal deployments .



- **[SEBLOD](https://github.com/Octopoos/SEBLOD)**  

  CCK (Content Construction Kit) for Joomla (license varies). Build high-end websites with custom content types and fields. 128 GitHub stars, 56 forks .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **[Joomla](https://github.com/joomla/joomla-cms)** — Open-source CMS (GPL-2.0) with multi-language support, ACL, and thousands of extensions for portal building.

- **[Drupal](https://github.com/drupal/drupal)** — Enterprise-grade open-source CMS (GPL-2.0) with robust content modeling for complex portals.

- **[WordPress](https://github.com/WordPress/WordPress)** — Most popular CMS (GPL-2.0) with themes and plugins for any portal use case.

- **[Strapi](https://github.com/strapi/strapi)** — Leading open-source headless CMS (MIT) for API-first portal content.

- **[Directus](https://github.com/directus/directus)** — Open-source data platform (BUSL) wrapping SQL databases with admin app and APIs.

- Community **n8n** workflows for portal automation and content orchestration.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Richie** or **Grav** as the portal CMS with **Gatewaze** for community features, **Strapi** or **Directus** for headless content APIs, and **PostgreSQL** for persistence — creating a fully self-hosted, enterprise-grade digital experience portal.
