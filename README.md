# Top Digital Experience Portal Builder Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Portal Creation, Community Building & No-Code Experience Design*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Digital Experience & Portal Builders**. These tools enable organizations to create branded portals, customer communities, self-service help centers, and partner websites — often with drag-and-drop builders and no-code workflows.

**Examples** include Salesforce Experience Cloud, Sitecore Experience Platform, Adobe Experience Manager, Liferay DXP, Webflow Enterprise, Bettermode, WordPress VIP, HubSpot Content Hub, Squarespace Enterprise, and Wix Studio (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom portal logic, and open experience management — ideal for enterprises, communities, and developers seeking transparent, vendor-independent portal solutions.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Salesforce Experience Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/products/experience-cloud/)**  
  Build branded help centers, communities, and portals connected to CRM data and processes. Features Experience Builder for drag-and-drop deployment, Lightning Bolt templates with industry-specific logic, AI-powered content recommendations via Einstein, and out-of-box Service Cloud integration for 360-degree customer views . Supports Q&A, groups, customer MVPs, and personalized feeds to turn customers into brand ambassadors .

- **[Sitecore Experience Platform](https://www.sitecore.com/platform/experience-platform)**  
  Market-leading all-in-one DXP for creating standout websites and omnichannel experiences. Features comprehensive marketing tools, holistic customer data view, and machine learning-generated insights. Deployment flexibility: on-premises/private cloud for data sovereignty, or Managed Cloud for scalability and performance . XP includes all XM capabilities plus robust personalization; XM is a lighter CMS with smaller infrastructure footprint .

- **[Adobe Experience Manager](https://business.adobe.com/products/experience-manager/sites/aem-sites.html)**  
  Enterprise DXP for creating and managing digital experiences. AEM Quick Site Creation enables non-developers to build sites from templates, while front-end developers customize themes without AEM backend knowledge . Edge Delivery Services is now recommended for new projects over traditional AEM site creation .

- **[Liferay DXP](https://www.liferay.com/)**  
  Enterprise portal solution for building customer portals, partner portals, supplier portals, and internal employee portals. Features CMS, commerce, AI, DAM, low-code tools, and search built-in . Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader for Digital Experience Platforms (2018–2021) and ranked #1 for B2E use cases in 2024 . Trusted by 1,200+ customers including Sumitomo Corporation, Yanmar, and Panasonic Connect .

- **[Webflow Enterprise](https://webflow.com/enterprise)**  
  Visual development platform for marketing teams to build and scale websites. Features visual development with design systems, native experiments, personalization, SEO/AEO, and localization . Built-in governance, workflows, and permissions enable speed without sacrificing control. 300,000+ brands use Webflow; customers report $6M+ annual savings and reduced time-to-market .

- **[Bettermode](https://bettermode.com/)**  
  Community platform for customer hubs. Features 52 capabilities across 14 categories: flexible Spaces, member authentication (email, social, SSO), visual Blocks customization, white-label with custom domain, app store integrations, engagement tools (reactions, leaderboards, badges), and advanced search . Used by IBM, ConvertKit, WebinarNinja, Condo Control, and CoachHub .

- **[WordPress VIP](https://wpvip.com/)**  
  Enterprise SaaS CMS built for B2B technology companies scaling from $50M to $1B ARR. Features Gutenberg block editor, built-in content analytics by Parse.ly, and managed infrastructure . Switch between single stack, headless, or hybrid without rearchitecting. 99.99% uptime during major events; SOC 2 Type I platform and Type II data centers .

- **[HubSpot Content Hub](https://www.hubspot.com/)**  
  CMS integrated with HubSpot CRM. Free tier includes website pages, blog, landing pages, and drag-and-drop editor. Professional adds smart content, A/B testing, and memberships; Enterprise adds multisites, content approvals, and custom objects .

- **[Squarespace Enterprise](https://www.squarespace.com/enterprise)**  
  Scalable website solution for brands managing multiple sites. Features global editing across sites, centralized dashboard for permissions and analytics, page drafts for collaborative editing, and team permissions by role .

- **[Wix Studio](https://www.wix.com/studio)**  
  Platform for agencies, freelancers, and enterprises to create high-end sites at scale. Features responsive AI, grid-based editor, real-time client feedback, version control, Figma plugin, and content mode for client editing without design impact .

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Richie](https://github.com/openfun/richie)**  
  Open-source CMS for building education portals (MIT). 316 GitHub stars, 95 forks. Built with Python/Django by openfun. Features course catalog management, organization pages, and person profiles. Actively maintained with 3,510 commits from 36 contributors .

- **[Gatewaze](https://github.com/gatewaze/gatewaze)**  
  Modular open-source platform for communities (license varies). Features members, events, content, and communications. Admin app at port 5274, public portal at port 3100. Setup wizard guides platform naming, administrator account creation, module selection, and theme choice. Supports Supabase (self-hosted or cloud) with Kubernetes Helm charts .

- **[Ibexa Experience](https://github.com/ibexa/experience)**  
  Modern modular DXP (license varies) for customer-centric organizations. Goes beyond content management to create websites and digital experiences . Ibexa Headless combines headless CMS, DAM, and PIM for API-first multichannel delivery; Ibexa Commerce is B2B DXP from customer awareness to purchase .

- **[sivujetti](https://github.com/sivujetti/sivujetti)**  
  Self-hosted website builder and CMS (license varies). Lightweight page builder for creating websites without SaaS dependencies .

- **[Visually](https://github.com/Git002/Visually)**  
  Drag-and-drop website builder that generates clean code (license varies). Gives users freedom to host their website anywhere. Built with Svelte .

- **[AntCMS](https://github.com/AntCMS-org/AntCMS)**  
  Super lightweight and simple CMS built for the modern web and simplicity (license varies) .

- **[p3ol/oak](https://github.com/p3ol/oak)**  
  Modern, lightweight and modulable page builder (license varies). 35 GitHub stars .

- **[Grav](https://github.com/getgrav/grav)**  
  Modern, fast flat-file CMS powered by PHP, Markdown, Twig, and Symfony (MIT). 14,260 GitHub stars, 1,376 forks. No database required, making it ideal for simple portal deployments .

- **[SEBLOD](https://github.com/Octopoos/SEBLOD)**  
  CCK (Content Construction Kit) for Joomla (license varies). Build high-end websites with custom content types and fields. 128 GitHub stars, 56 forks .

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **[Joomla](https://github.com/joomla/joomla-cms)** — Open-source CMS (GPL-2.0) with multi-language support, ACL, and thousands of extensions for portal building.
- **[Drupal](https://github.com/drupal/drupal)** — Enterprise-grade open-source CMS (GPL-2.0) with robust content modeling for complex portals.
- **[WordPress](https://github.com/WordPress/WordPress)** — Most popular CMS (GPL-2.0) with themes and plugins for any portal use case.
- **[Strapi](https://github.com/strapi/strapi)** — Leading open-source headless CMS (MIT) for API-first portal content.
- **[Directus](https://github.com/directus/directus)** — Open-source data platform (BUSL) wrapping SQL databases with admin app and APIs.
- Community **n8n** workflows for portal automation and content orchestration.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Richie** or **Grav** as the portal CMS with **Gatewaze** for community features, **Strapi** or **Directus** for headless content APIs, and **PostgreSQL** for persistence — creating a fully self-hosted, enterprise-grade digital experience portal.
